Fill Consistency / Relative Density from DCP blow counts

Uses the bands recovered from your DCP workbook (SAND: 0–2 Loose, 3–7 Medium Dense, 8–14 Dense, 15+ Very Dense). Anything you enter as a field assessment overrides it. Clay has no correlation available, so it stays blank.

Export files

Raw captured data for transcription into the Opifex templates. JSON has everything; CSVs are quick spreadsheet views.

Import a project

Takes a _fieldwork.json written by Export above — to move a job onto another phone, take over someone else’s holes, or put a job back after a wipe. A job already on this phone asks before anything is replaced.

The photographs themselves are not in the file — they stay on the phone that took them. Imported photo rows keep their captions, boreholes and depths, and print as blank boxes in the deck unless those images are already on this device.
